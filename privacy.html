Privacy Policy

Health Coach is a private, single-user app used only by its owner, Luke Loo.

It reads the owner's own Google Health / Fitbit data (such as sleep, heart rate, HRV and activity) with his permission, only to analyse his personal health trends. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
